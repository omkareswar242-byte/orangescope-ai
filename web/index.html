04 / METHODOLOGY

Two different ways to solve the task

Classical ML: SVM

OpenCV gets HSV/LAB color histograms and LBP texture features. StandardScaler and SVM use those values to predict a class.

Deep Learning: CNN

Images are resized and passed through four convolution blocks. The CNN learns patterns during training using PyTorch.
